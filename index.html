Welcome to Desert Haven

A home, not a facility

Desert Haven is a small, residential care home in Surprise where every resident is known by name. Our caregivers help with the everyday things, like meals, bathing, medications, and getting around, so residents can spend their days on what they enjoy.

When it's time to leave the house, our sister company Haven Ride handles the driving. One phone call covers care and transportation.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Don't see your question? Call us anytime at (623) 555-0123. Someone always answers.

Is someone really awake all night?

Yes. Desert Haven always has awake caregivers on site, every hour of every day, including weekends and holidays.

Can family visit anytime?

Absolutely. Families are part of our community. We just ask that late-night visitors call ahead so we can let you in quietly.

Do I have to live at Desert Haven to use Haven Ride?

No. Haven Ride serves any senior or person with mobility needs in Surprise and the surrounding West Valley. Residents get priority scheduling.

Do you accept long-term care insurance or ALTCS?

We work with many long-term care insurance plans and can help you understand your options. Call us to talk through your situation.

Can Haven Ride accommodate wheelchairs?

Yes. Our vans have wheelchair lifts and secure tie-downs, and drivers are trained to assist riders using walkers, canes, and wheelchairs.

How far ahead should I book a ride?

For routine trips, at least 24 hours ahead. Same-day rides are often available, so just call and ask.
